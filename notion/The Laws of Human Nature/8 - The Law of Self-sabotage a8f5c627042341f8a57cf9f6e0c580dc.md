# 8 - The Law of Self-sabotage

Propietario: Teo Chiappero

### Change Your Circumstances by Changing Your Attitude

By making our attitude more positive, open, and tolerant of other people, we can spark a different dynamic—we can learn from adversity, create opportunities out of nothing, and draw people to us.

> The greatest discovery of my generation is the fact that human beings can alter their lives by altering their attitudes of mind.
			—William James
> 

 The mind and the body are one, and your thoughts affect your physical responses. People can recover much more quickly from illness through sheer desire and willpower.

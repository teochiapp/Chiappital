# Fundamental Analysis

Propietario: Teo Chiappero

Some fundamental analysis can also be helpful for a swing trader and there is one factor that is very relevant. This involves watching for hot sector manias. In my opinion, it is one of the most powerful opportunities to make great returns on your money using fundamental analysis.

### Total Revenue

This revenue number is a measure of a company’s total sales of their products and/or services.

### Earnings per Share

Earnings are calculated by taking the total revenue and subtracting the direct costs of production. Positive earnings are important in the long term for any business to continue operating. However, the name “earnings” should not be confused with profit or profitability.

### Price to Earnings Ratio

You can use this number to compare how investors are valuing other companies in the same business sector. A higher P/E in relation to other companies in the same sector indicates that investors are feeling particularly bullish on the company.

### Debt to Equity

Financial people want to see a ratio of debt to equity of less than 1.

### Return on Equity

Return on equity (ROE) is a measure, expressed as a percentage, of how much profit a company generates with the money shareholders have invested.

### Hot Sector Manias

It is a well-known fact that catching a popular sector early is like grabbing a tiger by the tail. Once you grab one, hold on for the ride. I refer to this phenomenon as “hot sector manias” because all investing common sense seems to be abandoned by investors and traders.

In summary, sector mania plays are the one fundamental factor I like the most. The downside is that these do not occur very often and you will need to be patient but vigilant as you wait for the next hot sector trading opportunity to come along.

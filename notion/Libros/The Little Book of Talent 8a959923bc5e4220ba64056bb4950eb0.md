Hard skills are about repeatable precision, and tend to be found in specialized pursuits, particularly physical ones.

SOFT, HIGH-FLEXIBILITY SKILLS, on the other hand, are those that have many paths to a good result.With these skills, we are not trying for Swiss-watch precision, but rather for the ability to quickly recognize a pattern or possibility.

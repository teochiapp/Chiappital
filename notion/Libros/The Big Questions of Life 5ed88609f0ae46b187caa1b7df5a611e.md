# The Big Questions of Life

Propietario: Teo Chiappero
Etiquetas: Espiritualidad
Autor: Om Swami

Pain is inevitable, suffering is optional.

Separation from all that we love is not a question of ‘if’ but ‘when’

Whatever you seek from others, learn to give it to yourself first. To be selfless in love is to love the other person the way he or she wants to be loved.

The more responsible you are, the greater freedom you can have

decorate one’s inner house so richly that one is content there, glad to welcome anyone who wants to come and stay, but happy all the same when one is inevitably alone

Every Saturday since then, I have taken one marble out and thrown it away. I found that by watching the marbles diminish, I focused more on the really important things in life.

how do I change my inherent nature, so that I am able to react in a manner that befits me?

So that our sudden reactions to situations and people are replaced by thoughtful responses.

### *Five Strategies for Becoming a Master Persuader*

*1. Transform yourself into a deep listener*

*We hear parts of what other people are saying, in order to follow and keep the conversation going. At the same time, we’re planning what we’ll say next, some exciting story of our own. Or we are even daydreaming about something irrelevant*

*we are more interested in our own thoughts, feelings, and experiences than in those of the other person*

*Think of it this way: You know your own thoughts only too well. You are rarely surprised. Your mind tends to circle obsessively around the same subjects. But each person you encounter represents an undiscovered country full of surprises*

*For this purpose, you must not barrage them with questions that make it feel like a job interview.*

*You convey that you are listening by maintaining relatively consistent eye contact and nodding as they talk*

*The best way to signal how deeply you are listening is to occasionally say something that mirrors what they have said, but in your own words and filtered through your own experience.*

*2. Infect people with the proper mood*

*Complete indulgence. You do not judge other people; you accept them as they are*

*3. Confirm their self-opinion.*

*Intelligence. When you disagree with another person and impose your contrary opinion, you are implying that you know better*

*If you wish to win a man’s heart, allow him to confute you*

*You can create a similar effect by asking people for advice. The implication is that you respect their wisdom and experience.*

*Remind them of the good things they have done for you in the past. This will help confirm their self-opinion: “Yes, I am generous.”*

*4. Allay their insecurities*

*Everyone has particular insecurities*

*Once you’ve identified them, you must first be extra careful not to trigger them*

T*he best strategy is to praise and flatter those qualities that people are most insecure about*

*It is always better to praise people for their effort, not their talent. Everyone likes to feel that they earned their good fortune through hard work, and that is where you must aim your praise*

### Introduction

One of the many lessons I learned during that era was that it is easy to make money when prices are increasing (what’s called a bull market). The key to keeping your money is managing risk and always being vigilant on the mood or psychology of the market.

A good trader will only enter trades that are expected to give them at least 2 times the reward for the risk they are taking.

I view entering in a earnings reports more like gambling where you are at a roulette wheel betting on red or black. I do not like to gamble with my capital.

Knowing the exit prices before you enter the trade is critical to your risk

# Chapter 7: Selling Short

Propietario: Teo Chiappero

Although the market for stocks is upwardly biased when viewed over long periods, a good one-third of the time it's headed down. This also applies to individual stocks.

It's a statistical fact that stocks decline faster than they rise.  

Stocks fall apart much faster than they rise because fear causes a panic reaction while greed takes a while to simmer.' 

Just as the best stocks should be bought in a bull market, the most vulnerable ones should be sold short in a bear market.

Never forget this point: Don't ever short a stock that is above its rising 30-week MA.

S U M M A R Y O F SHORT-SELLING D O N ' T S

- Don't sell short because the P/E is too high.
- Don't sell short because the stock has run up too much.
- Don't sell short a sucker stock that everyone else agrees must crash.
- Don't sell short a stock that trades thinly.
- Don't sell short a Stage 2 stock.
- Don't sell short a stock in a strong group.
- Don't sell short without protecting yourself with a buy-stop order

TAKING SOME P R O F I T A B L E STEPS 

4. Relative Strength. Never sell a stock short that has very positive relative strength (RS), 

5. Volume. Now, pay careful attention! Volume is crucial when we're dealing with upside breakouts on the buy side. The short side is 100% different. While it's nice if volume does pick up significantly and confirm the downside breakout, volume is not a necessary ingredient for a winning short sale.

You don't need to make volume a major priority when you're looking for potential winners on the short side, while you most definitely do under the rules for buying. 

6. Support. The last factor to check out is how much nearby support is beneath the stock

Shorting well into a Stage 4 decline is a good trader's tactic because the selloffs that immediately follow are usually fast and furious. But this tactic is only to be used if a consolidation pattern forms beneath the declining MA and then a new breakdown occurs.

Be sure a consolidation has taken place, and a new breakdown occurs.  

The first ingredient of a valid head-and-shoulder top pattern is a stock which has been very strong and already had a substantial advance.

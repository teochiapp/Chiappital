# Meditations

Propietario: Teo Chiappero
Etiquetas: Filosofía
Autor: Marcus Aurelios
Calificación: 4

No one can hurt me but myself

Stop being dissatisfied with today and afraid of tomorrow.

If you don’t clear the clouds from your mind now, you may never have another chance.

Fill your mind with thoughts of love, freedom, and justice, leaving no room for negative and destructive thoughts.

Don’t worry about what others think. Mind what you think

Philosophy consists in keeping your inner spirit free, undisturbed, above pain and pleasure; In other words, living according to nature.

Put your whole heart and mind into your work, laboring not just for yourself but for the common good. Be a person of few words and a few projects, not busy and scattered.

Enjoy the present moment. Expect nothing, fear nothing, speak truly, and act heroically. No one can stop you.

When your thoughts are orderly and tranquil, there’s no place quieter and more peaceful than your own soul. There, you are free.

Look around you and see that everything that happens is the result of change. Nothing around you is in its final form.

Always take the shortest path—by which I mean the direct, natural route to your goal. Keep in line with reason and stay clear of all distractions, troubles, conflicts, deceptions, and ostentatious displays.

When it’s hard to get out of bed in the morning, remind yourself: I am rising to resume my life’s work.

If you truly love yourself, love your nature and your vocation.

Your character is simply the sum of your thoughts over time.

Did someone do me wrong? That’s their problem—they harmed their own character, not mine

For good fortune consists of a good attitude, good thoughts, and good actions.

Exert your efforts within your sphere of power, and be indifferent to everything else.

Listen carefully when others speak. As much as possible, imagine yourself in their shoes and see things from their perspective.

Don’t worry about the future. By doing your best today, you’ll build the strength and resources to handle whatever tomorrow may bring.

Stop with your idle daydreaming. Focus on the present. Perform the task at hand.

See things as they are, and don’t let your imagination blow them out of proportion.

As you improve your mind and disposition, your surroundings become more interesting, beautiful, and inspiring—no matter where you are.

Healthy eyes enjoy seeing all colors; healthy ears hear all sounds; a healthy nose takes in all scents.

A healthy mind, too, perceives all things, accepts all things, and makes use of all things.

So give up this futile pursuit of being liked by everyone. Simply live according to your principles

Far better to have an honest opponent than a false friend.

We’re all human beings. Why hate anyone, flatter anyone, lord over anyone, or bow before anyone?

“Each morning, remind yourself that this day may be your last.

Body and breath are yours to care for, while the mind is the part that does the caring. Mind is the gateway to your core self.

Our task is this: to play our role to the best of our ability, then depart in contentment and gratitude, praying only that that the Playwright is pleased with our performance.

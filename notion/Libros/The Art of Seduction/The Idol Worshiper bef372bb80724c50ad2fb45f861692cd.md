# The Idol Worshiper

Propietario: Teo Chiappero

Everyone feels an inner lack but Idol Worshipers have a bigger emptiness than most people. They cannot be satisfied with themselves, so they search the world for something to worship something to fill their inner void. This often assumes the form of a great interest in spiritual matters or in some worthwhile cause; by focusing on something supposedly elevated, they distract themselves from their own void, from what they dislike about themselves.

The way to seduce these types is to simply become their object of worship, to take the place of the cause or religion to which they are so dedicated

Keep two things in mind when seducing this type. First they tend to have overactive minds, which can make them quite suspicious

 Second, they often suffer from low self-esteem. Do not try to raise it; they will see through you, and your efforts at praising them will clash with their own self-image.

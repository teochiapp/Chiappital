# The Roué

Propietario: Teo Chiappero

These types have lived the good life and experienced many pleasures. They probably have, or once had, a good deal of money to finance their hedonistic lives

There is one type that can easily seduce them—the young and the innocent.

If you should want to seduce them, you will probably have to be somewhat young and to have retained at least the appearance of innocence. It is easy to play this up—make a show of how little experience you have in the world, how you still see things as a child.

# The Lonely Leader.

Propietario: Teo Chiappero

Powerful people are not necessarily different from everyone else, but they are treated differently, and this has a big effect on their personalities.

To seduce such types, it is better to act like their equal or even their superior—the kind of treatment they never get. If you are blunt with them you will seem genuine, and they will be touched—you care enough to be honest, even perhaps at some risk
